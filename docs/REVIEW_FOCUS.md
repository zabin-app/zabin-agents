# Portable Agent Contracts — Review Focus

## Review Priorities

1. **Conductor/worker privilege separation.** Reject any worker inventory, approval, role, or adapter change that admits conductor-only durable, destructive, verdict, gate, plan, wave, project, or graph operations. Cross-check the canonical [MCP policy](../config/zabin-mcp.json), its [schema](../schemas/mcp-policy.schema.json), and the [contract tests](../tests/test_contract_schemas.py).
2. **Credential and endpoint isolation.** The two surfaces must keep distinct URLs, client keys, and environment-backed credentials; literal credentials, cross-surface token reuse, secret-bearing diagnostics, or missing-secret network activity are security failures. Trace [rendering](../scripts/render_adapters.py), [installation](../scripts/install_adapters.py), [readiness](../scripts/zabin_doctor.py), and [native-host evidence](../tests/conformance/test_native_hosts.py) together.
3. **Lease and status ownership.** Compare lifecycle changes with the [public lifecycle contract](../skills/conductor/references/mcp-lifecycle.md): workers stop at review handoff and retain the lease, while terminal verdict, merge, gate, completion, and release decisions remain conductor-owned under the same worker identity.
4. **Authoritative state and recovery.** Zabin reads precede local recovery, and checkpoints may fill only explicit read-model gaps. A checkpoint conflict, guessed identity/base, automatic checkpoint upload, or local Markdown ledger must fail closed; use the [recovery contract](../skills/conductor/references/recovery.md) and [checkpoint schema](../schemas/recovery-checkpoint.schema.json).
5. **Declared scope and commit lineage.** Every changed and committed path must be in the task's authoritative write set; source, recorded, and merged SHAs must reconcile before completion. Review the containment and ownership boundaries in [wave implementation](../skills/conductor/workflows/implement-wave.md) and the [recovery mapping contract](../skills/conductor/references/recovery.md).
6. **Generated adapter policy and transactional install.** Policy, templates, rendered artifacts, installer ownership, drift reports, backups, and rollback must remain one coherent update. Pay special attention when a change touches both [renderer](../scripts/render_adapters.py) and [installer](../scripts/install_adapters.py), with their [render](../tests/test_render_adapters.py) and [install](../tests/test_install_adapters.py) regression evidence.
7. **Protocol and server identity.** Initialization revision, service/build identity, surface, exact inventory, and fingerprint must match before an adapter or conformance process treats a connection as Zabin. The pins live in the [runner lock](../tests/conformance/runner-lock.json); probing lives in [readiness](../scripts/zabin_doctor.py) and the [conformance runner](../scripts/run_conformance.py).
8. **Conformance claims.** A full-pass claim requires separately scored conductor and worker results plus native-host, lifecycle, checkpoint, artifact, dependency, cleanup, denial-receipt, and redaction evidence. Missing, skipped, stale, expected-failure, unscored, or shared-surface evidence remains failure under the [conformance evidence policy](../tests/conformance/README.md).
9. **PI remains unsupported.** Any PI enablement or support claim must satisfy every blocking re-evaluation condition and update the audit, immutable extension lock, empty settings gate, and runner lock consistently. Review [compatibility](../adapters/pi/COMPATIBILITY.md), [extension evidence](../adapters/pi/extension-lock.json), [disabled settings](../adapters/pi/settings.json.template), and the [unsupported-gate tests](../tests/test_pi_adapter.py).
10. **Documentation boundaries and precedence.** Managed-doc changes must preserve the canonical owner for each fact, root/path-scoped instruction precedence, the import-only wrapper, size limits, and valid links. Gate root instructions, `CLAUDE.md`, and the [managed docs](ARCHITECTURE.md) against the [documentation schema](../skills/doc-validate/schemas.md).

## Severity Calibration

- **Critical:** A change can expose conductor-only authority through the worker surface; disclose or cross-wire a credential; relax endpoint or identity pins so an untrusted process receives it; permit a forbidden call to reach transport; accept PI as supported without clearing every gate; or merge, complete, release, or recover work under the wrong project/task/lease identity or from non-authoritative state. Mixed installed policy after a failed rollback is also Critical when it weakens an active authorization boundary.
- **Major:** A change breaks declared write containment or commit mapping; loses a live lease/status handoff; leaves adapter drift or rollback incomplete without demonstrating active privilege expansion; accepts protocol/build/inventory mismatch; or presents conformance as passing without all required independent evidence. A managed-doc boundary, precedence, wrapper, size, or link failure that can misdirect implementation or review is Major.
- **Minor:** Project-specific diagnostic, provenance, evidence-label, or documentation-pointer drift that does not change authorization, credential handling, lifecycle state, recovery authority, installed output, support status, or pass/fail outcome. Escalate any apparently cosmetic issue when it masks one of those outcomes.

## Boundaries

- System shape belongs in [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md), implementation conventions in [Code Standards](CODE_STANDARDS.md), and all setup or verification commands in [Development](DEVELOPMENT.md); review findings should link there instead of restating them.
- Tool inventories, lifecycle algorithms, recovery formats, installation procedures, and PI audit detail remain canonical in the linked policy, schema, workflow, and compatibility sources above.
